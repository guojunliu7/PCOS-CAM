# Figure 3: exploratory cell-state evidence

`RawData/Figure_3_validation.R` reproduces the figure and the two expression-derived CSV files from the retained `query_input.rds` (4,913 cells from GSE268919). It also rebuilds `ten_cluster_prediction_audit.csv` from the saved PCOS-CAM, ScType, and SingleR predictions. Outputs are `Figures_Tables/Figure_3.pdf` and `RawData/Figure_3.png`.

Panel a recomputes a UMAP from the top 1,000 variable genes of the sampled expression matrix (20 PCs; seed 17021). It is not the original study embedding. Panel b shows the fraction of cells with nonzero expression and log2 enrichment against all other sampled cells, with a 0.05 pseudocount. Panel c shows cluster means for the granulosa comparison and discordant cluster 13. All displayed expression values come from the same test dataset. The selected genes overlap the markers used to assign local reference labels, so this is **within-dataset phenotype evidence**, not independent validation or a formal accuracy estimate.

`fine_label_review.csv` records the most relevant subtype-level interpretations, including partial support and conflicts. The results show granulosa state patterns (Amh in 1, Cyp19a1/Inhba/Nppc in 7, and cell-cycle genes in 8), but cannot establish a Mantri mural subtype, the exact Zhao pGC/ProGC identities, or a one-to-one mapping between the SingleR ovarian reference labels and the local labels. Cluster 13 has stromal Dcn alongside cycling genes, explaining why a ProGC call is problematic. Cluster 20 has B-cell Ms4a1/Cd79a despite a PCOS-CAM Phagocytes B label. Thus, the Figure 2 finer-label counts should remain measures of output resolution, not counts of confirmed correct subtypes.

For independent accuracy assessment, freeze dictionary, mapping and candidate rules; choose a held-out PCOS cohort with granular expert labels; remove any study used in dictionary construction from the test set; then assess broad-family agreement, fine-label precision/recall, and unassigned coverage per method.
