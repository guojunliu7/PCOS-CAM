# External transfer test: GSE240688

GSE240688 is a human granulosa-cell scRNA-seq GEO series with three PCOS and three control samples. Its submitters (Su, Li and Xie) are not among the 17 named dictionary studies. Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE240688. The local Seurat object contains 23,869 cells across 15 clusters and was previously reannotated using marker expression. Those refined labels are **local interpretations of the same dataset**, not author-verified subtype ground truth. No model setting was changed after looking at this test set.

`run.R` samples up to 40 cells per sample-cluster block (seed 17021), yielding 2,508 cells. It extracts full-transcriptome normalized RNA expression, converts to linear scale for PCOS-CAM's cluster mean scoring, and runs the frozen hierarchy (two positive marker hits; relative lead 0.10). SingleR uses `celldex::HumanPrimaryCellAtlasData` fine labels with cluster-level pruned calls. `run_sctype.R` runs pinned official ScType database and automatic tissue selection on the identical cells; ScType selected **Hippocampus**. Its Seurat 5 layer accessor was adapted as in the prior benchmark. Both reference methods retain their own non-ovarian default resources. The detailed inputs and predictions are saved here.

`RawData/Figure_4_external_validation.py` maps the labels to broad families and plots `Figures_Tables/Figure_4.pdf` and `RawData/Figure_4.png`. Seven high-confidence local Granulosa/Immune clusters (0, 1, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13) were selected. Broad-family concordance with those local labels was **2/7 PCOS-CAM, 1/7 SingleR, 2/7 ScType**. PCOS-CAM and ScType therefore had the same observed broad-family match count, both numerically higher than SingleR in this cohort. For ScType, the two matches are brain-specific microglial labels mapped to the broad Immune family; they do not establish ovarian immune subtype accuracy. The 8 remaining clusters and every raw label are retained in `external_prediction_audit.csv`.

PCOS-CAM frequently selected `Theca & stroma 4 (Wu)` for granulosa clusters. `diagnose.R` produced `PCOS_CAM_parent_contribution_audit.csv`: in cluster 0, SPARC supplied 64.8% of the predicted Theca/stroma family score and IGFBP5 another 21.6%. The parent call was unstable to removing the top-contributing gene. This suggests broad stromal genes dominate the current weighted score in this cohort. It is a diagnostic observation, not a corrected or retuned result. Cluster 13 (inflammatory granulosa) and cluster 12 (immune) are PCOS-CAM's two family matches in the seven-cluster subset.

The source cohort is enriched for granulosa cells, and the local reference labels were inferred from this cohort itself. These numbers measure **descriptive transfer agreement**, not independent subtype accuracy. The results contradict a general claim of PCOS-CAM superiority across cohorts. A stronger assessment requires independent expert subtype labels and a second ovarian tissue cohort, plus prespecified handling of mixed stromal/granulosa states. The present external data can guide algorithm redesign, but any redesign must be evaluated on a separate untouched cohort.
